# Sprint 4 wellness usability session

## Session setup

- Allow 15–20 minutes; ask the participant to think aloud.
- Use a demo account with no real health data and a device with the Sprint 4 build.
- Do not ask participants to change prescribed fluid limits or activity plans.
- Record completion, hesitation, misunderstood labels, and any accessibility issue. Do not record names or medical details.

## Tasks

1. From Home, find today's habit score and explain what you think it measures.
2. Open Wellness and add 250 ml of water. Explain what the water number and estimate mean.
3. Log 1,200 steps and find the updated total and progress toward the step goal.
4. Log a 10-minute walk and explain how it appears in today's activity.
5. With a prepared demo profile that has a fluid restriction, find the water guidance. Ask what the participant would do if their clinician gave them a different limit.
6. With network access disabled, add a habit entry. Ask what the offline message means and where the entry is saved.
7. Restore network access, refresh, and confirm the entry appears as synced without being counted twice.

## Follow-up questions

- Which goal, label, or message was unclear?
- Did any number feel like medical advice or conflict with guidance from a clinician?
- Could you tell whether an entry was saved locally or synced?
- What would make this screen easier to use with larger text or a screen reader?

## Completion notes

Run this script with 5–10 participants during Sprint 8 usability testing. Summarize patterns and issues without including identifying or medical information.
